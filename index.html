Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Primeline Products Philippines, Inc. ("Primeline," "we," "us," or "our") values the privacy of our employees. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data submitted through the HR Service Portal, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

Information We Collect

When you use the portal, we may collect personal information such as your name, employee ID, position, department, contact details, and any information you provide when submitting requests, forms, or letters (e.g., certificates, benefits, or compensation requests).

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to process HR-related requests, generate official documents, verify employment details, administer benefits, and improve the services offered through this portal. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal information based on:
• Your consent
• Performance of a contract
• Compliance with legal obligations
• Our legitimate business interests

Data Sharing

Primeline does not sell personal information. Information may be shared internally with authorized HR personnel, and externally with government agencies (such as SSS, Pag-IBIG, and PhilHealth) only where required to process statutory benefits or as mandated by law.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to:
• fulfill the purposes stated in this policy or as required by law
• We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. For concerns, please contact your HR department.

Acceptable Use

This portal is intended solely for legitimate HR-related transactions by authorized Primeline employees. Any misuse, falsification of information, or unauthorized access will be subject to disciplinary action under company policy.

Accuracy of Requests

You are responsible for ensuring that all information submitted through requests, forms, and letters is true, accurate, and complete. Primeline reserves the right to verify, delay, or deny requests containing inaccurate or incomplete information.

Data Collection & Consent

By checking the agreement box below and continuing to use this portal, you acknowledge and consent that we collect, process, and store your personal and employment data as described in our Privacy Policy, for the purpose of delivering HR services to you.

Changes to These Terms
